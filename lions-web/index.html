Mission

Make what a student can actually do visible, verifiable and arguable.

So the first question in campus hiring is what you built — not how well you described it.

Vision

A campus labour market where verified evidence outweighs where you studied, whom you know, or how your CV is formatted.

One profile, portable across departments, read on the same terms by a lab lead in a smaller city and an engineering manager in a metropolis.

01 · The solution

A verification layer, not another job board.

A first-round screen is a formatting contest today. The signal already exists in a student's work — it has no structure a buyer can read. We give it one.

03 · Non-negotiables

Built to be refused.

A verification system can be abused. These four lines are the reason it should be trusted — and we will not cross them for revenue.

  • Evidence over adjectives.If it cannot be opened, run or verified, it is not a qualification — it is a claim.
  • Explainable by default.A result nobody can argue with is a result nobody should trust.
  • The student owns the read.Public repositories only, after authorisation. No names, no transcripts, no browsing history, no contact graph.
  • We rank; a human decides.We will not sell automation that removes a person from a decision about a person.
